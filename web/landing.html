Deployment protocol

Onboard any company in three layers.

Nothing company-specific lives in code. A law firm, a clinic, and a claims processor differ in nouns; the skills are the verbs.

The universal engine

The 12 skills, the graph schema, and the decision policy: cite everything, compute confidence from evidence, never settle a live disagreement.
